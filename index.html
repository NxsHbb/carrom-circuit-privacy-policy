Privacy Policy

Carrom Circuit  ·  Last updated 10 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you play Carrom Circuit, an offline carrom board game for Android.

The short version. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us, and we hold no information about you whatsoever. Your coins, settings and progress are saved only on your own device.

The one exception is advertising: the game shows ads supplied by Google AdMob, and Google collects information in order to serve and measure them. That is described in full below.

Who we are

Carrom Circuit is published by ItemCoder. You can reach us at itemcoder@gmail.com with any question about this policy or about information relating to you.

Information we collect

None. We do not operate a server, an account system or an analytics service. The game never transmits your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, files or gameplay to us, because there is nowhere for it to send them.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally so it is there when you come back. This never leaves your phone, is not readable by other apps, and is deleted when you uninstall the game. It consists of:

  • your coin balance and the highest balance you have reached;
  • your settings — sound, vibration, the aim guide, the turn timer and the time allowed for each shot, and whether the board turns round between players;
  • which equipment you own and which is in play;
  • which prize tables you have won at;
  • your daily reward streak and the time of your last free coin refill;
  • counters that limit how often ads appear.

None of this identifies you. There is no way to move it to another device, and no way for us to see it.

Advertising

The game shows two kinds of ad, both supplied by Google AdMob: a full-screen ad shown occasionally after a match against the computer, and an optional video you may choose to watch in exchange for in-game coins. You are never required to watch an ad to keep playing.

To serve and measure those ads, Google collects information through its advertising SDK, which may include:

  • your device's advertising ID, a resettable identifier Android provides for this purpose;
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system version, language and the app's identifier;
  • your approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • information about ad interactions, such as whether an ad was shown, viewed or clicked.

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices about advertising

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any personalised advertising, using Google's official consent interface. You can change your answer at any time from Settings → Advertising choices inside the game. That row appears when Google's SDK reports that privacy options apply to you, which is the case in those regions.
  • On any device, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops personalised advertising; you will still see ads, but they will not be chosen based on your advertising ID.
  • Full-screen ads follow matches against the computer only. If you play two-player games on one device and never choose a reward video, you will not be shown an ad at all.

Permissions

The game itself requests no Android permissions. The permissions listed on the Play Store page are required by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, and are used only for advertising:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to fetch ads and to tell whether a connection is available;
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — to read the advertising ID described above;
  • ACCESS_ADSERVICES_* — for Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising APIs;
  • WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE — used internally by Google Play services while handling ad requests.

The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, files or any other sensitive permission, and it cannot read them.

Coins and purchases

Coins in Carrom Circuit are virtual, have no monetary value, and cannot be bought, sold, cashed out or transferred. There are no in-app purchases, and the game never asks for payment details. Nothing in the game is gambling: entries and prizes are play money only.

Children

Carrom Circuit is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, please contact us and we will act on it — though, as described above, we hold no information at all.

Data retention and deletion

Because we store nothing, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Everything the game saves is on your device, and uninstalling the game removes all of it. To have Google remove advertising data, use the Android advertising settings described above and Google's own privacy tools.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it, and the updated policy is published here. Material changes will also be noted in the app's Play Store listing.
